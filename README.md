## 🏔️ About Kahol Labs

**[Kahol Labs](https://kahollabs.in)** is an independent software development and digital engineering studio rooted in **Himachal Pradesh, India**. Founded by **Abhinadan Kahol** and drawing our lineage from the heritage village of **Mawa Kaholan (Una district)**, we bring honest craftsmanship, disciplined engineering, and grounded problem-solving into modern technology.

We design and engineer dependable mobile applications, cloud backend architectures, high-performance web platforms, and applied AI automation for businesses across **Mohali, Chandigarh, Punjab, Delhi NCR (Gurgaon, Noida)**, all 12 districts of **Himachal Pradesh**, and global partners.

## 🛠️ Core Engineering Disciplines

Explore our specialized software engineering capabilities:

* **[Native Android App Development](https://kahollabs.in/services/android-app-development):** Modern Android development with Kotlin 2.2, Jetpack Compose, Room SQLite, and Clean Architecture.
* **[iOS App Development](https://kahollabs.in/services/ios-app-development):** Native iOS applications crafted in Swift, SwiftUI, Combine, and Apple HIG.
* **[Cross-Platform Flutter & React Native](https://kahollabs.in/services/flutter-development):** Shared codebases with deliberate platform-specific device performance.
* **[Backend Architecture & Scalable APIs](https://kahollabs.in/services/backend-api-development):** Resilient REST & GraphQL APIs built with Node.js, TypeScript, Python, PostgreSQL, and Firebase.
* **[Applied AI & Autonomous Workflows](https://kahollabs.in/ai):** Multi-step AI agents, intelligent document processing (IDP), knowledge-grounded RAG systems, and custom LLM integrations.

* **[UI/UX Product Design](https://kahollabs.in/services/ui-ux-design):** Human-centered design systems, WCAG accessibility, and high-fidelity prototypes.
